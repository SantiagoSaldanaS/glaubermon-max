Welcome! You’ve been challenged to battle **`Glaubermax`**—an autonomous, Grandmaster-level AI competitive Pokémon bot powered by **Deep Reinforcement Learning (AlphaZero/ReBeL)**, game theory Nash equilibria, and instant tree search.

This guide takes you step-by-step from zero to your first match in **under 2 minutes**—no downloads or prior competitive experience needed!

## 🚀 Quick Start in 4 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Open Pokémon Showdown
1. Open your web browser on PC, Mac, iPad, or mobile.
2. Go to: **[https://play.pokemonshowdown.com](https://play.pokemonshowdown.com)**
*(Showdown runs completely in your browser; no installation needed).*

---

### Step 2: Choose a Nickname
1. In the top-right corner of the screen, click the **"Choose name"** button.
2. Type any username you'd like (e.g., `TrainerAsh99`).
3. If the name is taken, pick another one. Once in, you can click the **Gear (⚙️ Settings)** icon or **"Register"** to set a password so your name is saved for future battles.

---

### Step 3: Import a Ready-to-Play Team Preset
You need a competitive 6-Pokémon team for the standard format (**`[Gen 9] OU`**). We've prepared 3 battle-ready, tournament-winning teams for you below.

1. On the left navigation menu, click **"Teambuilder"**.
2. Click the green **"+ New Team"** button.
3. Click the **"Import from text"** button at the top.
4. Copy the entire text of **one** of the teams below (Team 1 is recommended for your first game) and paste it into the text box.
5. Click the green **"Save"** button at the top.
6. In the **"Format"** dropdown at the top, select **`[Gen 9] OU`**.
7. Click the **"← Teams"** back arrow. Your team is ready!

---

### Step 4: Challenge the Bot (`Glaubermax`)
1. In the right-hand panel, click the **"Find a user"** button.
2. Type in **`Glaubermax`** and press **Enter**.
3. A popup profile card will appear. Click the green **"Challenge"** button.
4. Set the options in the challenge prompt:
   - **Format**: `[Gen 9] OU`
   - **Team**: Select the team you just imported.
5. Click **"Challenge"**!
6. **`Glaubermax` will automatically and instantly accept your challenge!** The battle arena will load immediately.

## ⚔️ How In-Battle Decisions Work (Beginner Guide)

### 1. Team Preview (Turn 0)
At the start of the battle, both players see each other's 6 Pokémon. Click on the Pokémon you want to send out **first** (the "Lead"). 
- *Tip*: **Great Tusk** or **Samurott-Hisui** are great starting leads to immediately threaten the opponent or lay entry hazards.

### 2. Making Your Turn
Each turn, you can do one of two things:
1. **Click an Attack (Moves 1–4)**: Select one of your 4 moves to attack or set up.
2. **Switch Pokémon**: Click on any healthy Pokémon on your bench below the move buttons to swap them in.

### 3. Terastallization (Tera)
- **What is it?** Once per battle, you can change your active Pokémon's type into its special **Tera Type**!
- **How to use it?** Click the **"Terastallize"** checkbox right above your moves *before* clicking your attack.
- **Why use it?**
  - **Defensively**: If your Kingambit (Dark/Steel) is about to get hit by a 4x super-effective Fighting move, Terastallizing into **Flying** makes that Fighting move deal only 0.5x resisted damage and makes you immune to Ground moves!
  - **Offensively**: Terastallizing into your move's type powers it up for massive bonus damage.

### 4. Battle Timer
- The bot activates a **120-second timer** (`requested by Glaubermax`) so turns resolve promptly. If you don't make a move within the time limit, Showdown will make an automatic move for you.

## 🧠 Pro Tips to Beat the Bot

1. **Watch Out for Sucker Punch**:
   - Kingambit's `Sucker Punch` moves with **+1 Priority** (strikes before you can move), BUT it only works if you are using an attacking move! If you use a Status move (like `Swords Dance` or `Spiky Shield`), Sucker Punch fails.
2. **Gholdengo's Good as Gold**:
   - Gholdengo is immune to all status moves (Thunder Wave, Toxic, Will-O-Wisp, Whirlwind). Don't waste turns trying to status Gholdengo!
3. **Popping Air Balloons**:
   - Gholdengo often starts with an `Air Balloon`, granting total immunity to Ground attacks (Earthquake / Headlong Rush). Hit it with *any* damaging attack first to pop the balloon, and Ground moves will work normally again.
4. **Beware Supreme Overlord**:
   - Kingambit grows stronger for every fallen teammate (+10% Attack per fainted ally). If Kingambit is the last Pokémon standing, its attacks deal devastating damage!
5. **Entry Hazards**:
   - Setting `Stealth Rock` or `Spikes` chips the bot's Pokémon every time they switch in.

## ❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

- **Q: What if Glaubermax doesn't accept my challenge?**
  - Make sure the format is set to **`[Gen 9] OU`**. The bot only accepts Gen 9 OU battles.
  - If the bot is currently in a ladder match, wait 1–2 minutes for its game to finish, and it will accept immediately.
- **Q: Can I spectate Glaubermax's matches?**
  - Yes! Search `Glaubermax` in Showdown, click the profile, and click **"Watch Battle"** to watch the bot play in real-time.
- **Q: Can I use my own custom team?**
  - Absolutely! As long as the team complies with standard **[Gen 9] OU** rules (no banned Ubers like Koraidon, Miraidon, or Calyrex), the bot will happily battle you.
